The Property

A contemporary loft in the heart of Palermo

Urban Retreat is a spacious renovated apartment in Palermo's historic centre, featuring exposed wooden beam ceilings — typical of the attic spaces in Sicilian palazzi — reinterpreted with a modern touch. The open-plan space combining kitchen, dining and living areas is designed to welcome groups of friends and large families in total comfort.

The apartment has two spacious bedrooms, each with the option to add a single bed, and two en-suite bathrooms finished in dark marble with a shower. The living area also features a comfortable queen sofa bed, ideal for hosting up to six people without compromising on space.

The location is strategic: just steps from Palermo Cathedral, the Teatro Massimo and the historic Vucciria and Ballarò markets. To plan your days in the city, check out our guide to things to do in Palermo and our tips on where to stay in the historic centre.

Also worth exploring: the best restaurants in Palermo, the most beautiful beaches near Palermo and luxury experiences in Sicily.

Frequently Asked Questions

Urban Retreat – FAQ

How many guests can Urban Retreat accommodate?
The apartment comfortably sleeps up to 6 guests thanks to two double bedrooms and a spacious sofa bed in the living room. Extra single beds are available on request for larger groups.
Is parking available at Urban Retreat?
The apartment is located within Palermo's limited traffic zone (ZTL) in the historic centre. We recommend the partner private garage just a few metres from the building's entrance.
How far is it from Palermo's main landmarks?
The location is extremely central: the Cathedral is about 250m away, Quattro Canti 300m and the Teatro Massimo around 450m. Everything is reachable on a short walk.
Is the Wi-Fi connection fast enough for working?
Yes, the loft has dedicated high-speed fibre Wi-Fi, perfect for remote work and video calls.
How does check-in work?
We offer a personalised welcome service with flexible hours. For arrivals after 8:00 PM, please let us know in advance so we can best organise your check-in.
